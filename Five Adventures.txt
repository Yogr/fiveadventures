Game Design
-----------------
I want to make a web game called Five Adventures. This is a daily Role-Playing Game web app. The idea is that we will allow users to have only Five Adventures a day. Each adventure will be something random from a list of various adventures that they can have. Some of these may be bad adventures, good adventures, or adventures that could end with a good or bad outcome. Players will start by creating a character, choosing a class from up to four class types: Warrior, Wizard, Thief, or Ranger. They can also give their character a name, or press a randomize button that will generate a name for them. The game will feature a pixelated art style that is light hearted, cute and fun. After making a character, they can then begin an adventure. There will be an adventure tracker showing 1->2->3->4->5, tracking the day's five adventures. Users will only be allowed one character for their account by default, but we should store these in a separate database table and have an associative table for their user profile. As a result of each adventure, the user will collect experience points, gold, and sometimes an item. Characters have inventories, allowing them to equip an item for each of the following categories: Weapon, Helmet, Armor, Trinket. These items will give them stats and benefits to help boost their adventure success outcomes on future adventures. Here are the following stats that the characters will have: Strength, Intelligence, Agility, and Luck. Characters will also have Hitpoints and Energy. Each day, their hitpoints and energy refresh. If a character loses all of their hitpoints before reaching their final adventure, their next adventure must be something that is non-violent, meaning they cannot lose more hitpoints, likely they stumble on a healing fountain or a magic bean or something that may have an effect that can benefit them in some way but not as rewarding as a full adventure. At the end of the adventures, they can do one final action: Fight the World Boss. This is a global weekly enemy that will be attacked collectively by all players playing the game. Each day, after the five adventures, attacking the world boss will deal damage to it and this will scale based on the player's level and stats, equipment, and remaining hitpoints (They will get X number of attacks multiplier based on remaining hp. if 1 hp remaining, they get just one attack.). We must store the amount of damage each character has done to each weekly boss. If the weekly boss is defeated, rewards will be issued to all characters that participated in the world boss fight by contributing at least 1 damage. There will be reward tiers with the most legendary and exclusive items going to the top tier contributors, and everyone else will get lower tier rewards but with a lottery system to have a chance at the top tier based on how many days they contributed, regardless of the damage they dealt. These rewards should be viewable by clicking a reward info button next to the world boss encounter display. In addition to this, there will also be a shop that the user can spend their gold on, or sell their items they no longer need. This shop will have 8 random items that are the same for all players, but each character has their own instance of the shop so they each have the ability to buy those items. The shop items will refresh daily. Users can use the shop before or after any adventure. They can also access and change their inventory or equipped items before or after any adventure. When finding an item of a category slot they do not have anything equipped in yet, those items will auto-equip. Each adventure may be approached differently by each character class. For instance, abilities and stats should be factored in to the potential outcomes of an adventure. Maybe if a townsfolk has a tree that has fallen on them, one option may be available to characters that have more than 10 strength to attempt to lift the tree. The success of the attempted lift goes up the higher their strength is beyond 10, with it being a guaranteed success at some threshold. Warriors have more strength and a bit more agility, Wizards have more intelligence and a bit more luck, Thieves have more luck and a bit more agility, and Rangers have more agility and balanced strength, intelligence, and luck, by default. Weapons all have a base damage value. Armor and Helmets have a base defense value. Trinkets only have random effects. Armor, Helmets, and Weapons also can all have random effects. Weapons also have weapon types, some weapon types are more useful in some encounters such as needing to chop tall grass (slashing) or needing to smash something (blunt). 

Design specifics:
- Lots of Equipment and items
- Lots of different, fun Adventures, often with multiple Decisions and variable Outcomes for each Decision
- Each adventure has a minimum experience value that is rewarded, but can reward more per the Outcome exp bonus
- Adventures often have Outcomes determined by character specifics
- 4 Character classes
- Weekly world Boss

Specifics of the game development:
- Website is five-adventures.com
- Using Vercel, NextJs, React, TypeScript, and Supabase
- We need a login system with easy one-click login provider associations from Google, Apple/iOs, Facebook
- Users do not need to login to play, and we should utilize their cookies or local browser cache to save their character ID to fetch their character, however if they are not logged in we should recommend them to create an account or else they may lose their progress. Once an account is created, we need to link their user ID with their character. Each character that is unlinked should have a status of 'unlinked'.
- We need to make sure to use the Server clock to determine when the next 'day' is ready. We need to track each character's daily adventure count, and the last day that the character played. The server should count the first date of launch as day '1', and increment every day beyond that. If a character's last played day is 5 and the server determines the current day is 6, then we should reset the character's daily adventure count to 0 to allow them to do more adventures.
- Adventures should be randomly generated differently for each character, but this seed should also be created for each new day 'reset' for each character, so that it can't change by refreshing the web browser or anything else.
- Make sure we track each characters weekly boss progress with a single row per character that gets updated, tracking each week as week '1' for days 1-7, week '2' for days 8-14, etc. This way when we load the rewards for each character we can just look at their individual progress in terms of number of times they attacked the world boss (limited at 1 per day) and how much damage they've done.
- We must track if the user has seen their weekly rewards from the previous boss. It should be displayed upon the first time they log back in following the ending of the prior week where they received rewards, but not after that. The rewards can be determined at the time they log in, if they have a 'pending rewards' flag set, which should be paired with a 'pending reward week' value to determine which week to look up to see the last time they contributed to a boss. We also need to track the World boss progress in its own table, tabulating its total hitpoints, total number of players that have attacked, number of times its been attacked, and amount of damage that has been done to it, and what week the boss represents.
- Items and Adventure data should be stored in such a way that it is easy to fetch, lookup, and add or remove new items, adventures, outcomes, etc to.
